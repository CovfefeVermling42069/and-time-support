Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

And... Time! is a timer for speeches. It does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

The speeches you create, with their names, times, and sound choices, are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere, and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create accounts or ask for personal information.
  • It makes no network connections.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
