Sounds

The chime, ding, and buzzer sounds are adapted from these recordings from Freesound, each licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0):

  • “Large Wind Chime 2 (D6/86)” by iainmccurdy — freesound.org/s/696635
  • “Quiz Show Buzzer 1 (8-bit)” by JapanYoshiTheGamer — freesound.org/s/423220
  • “buzzer_boardgame_02.wav” by magedu — freesound.org/s/395806

The recordings were edited for this app: they were shortened, faded out, adjusted in volume, and converted to the app’s audio format. The other sounds in the app are synthesized by the app’s author.
