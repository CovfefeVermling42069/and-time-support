Need help?

And... Time! is a speech timer for public speaking classes. If something is not working, or you have an idea, open an issue on GitHub or reach out through my GitHub profile. Please say which device and iOS version you have, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

I do not hear the sounds.

The timer plays through the speaker even when the silent switch is on, so check the volume first. In the speech editor, tap the play button next to a time to hear its sound, and make sure that time is switched on and has a sound chosen.

The screen did not change color at a time.

Each time (minimum, target, penalty, and hard stop) has its own switch in the speech editor. A time that is switched off is skipped.

Are my speeches backed up?

Speeches are stored only on your device, so they are not synced or backed up by the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is the time accurate if I leave the app?

Yes. The timer measures from the clock, so the time is correct when you come back. Sounds are not played late for times that passed while the app was in the background.

What do I need to run it?

An iPad or iPhone running iPadOS or iOS 17 or later.
